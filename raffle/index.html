Clear From the Start

How It Will Work

  • Complete prize information will be posted as prizes are confirmed.
  • Ticket pricing and the sales period will be announced before sales open.
  • Every confirmed prize will include its description, donor, approximate retail value when available, and any important restrictions or expiration date.
  • Everyone will be able to review the Official Raffle Rules before purchasing tickets.
  • Winners and winner-claim information will be posted or communicated after the drawing in accordance with the Official Raffle Rules.
  • The raffle will be conducted by the Kirtland Heritage Group in accordance with applicable Ohio law.

A Stronger Northeast Ohio

Where the Proceeds Go

Raffle proceeds will help the Kirtland Heritage Group continue bringing people together through community programs, historical initiatives, interfaith events, charitable outreach, and other efforts that strengthen Northeast Ohio.

Community Partners

Help Us Build an Incredible Raffle

We are inviting businesses, attractions, restaurants, hotels, entertainment venues, sports organizations, service providers, and generous community members to donate memorable prizes.

Possible donations include gift cards, products, services, tickets, travel or hotel stays, dining experiences, family activities, golf packages, wellness packages, merchandise, complete themed baskets, or other unique experiences.

Selected raffle donors may be recognized through the prize display, NEOChosen.com, Kirtland Heritage Group social media, event signage, and other promotional materials based on the nature and value of the contribution.

Donor recognition is promotional acknowledgment only. It does not change the raffle rules or affect any participant’s odds of winning.
