## Shared characteristics of professional event websites

1. The first viewport establishes **identity, date and experience** before administrative details.
2. Brand typography has personality, while schedules, venues and actions use readable functional type.
3. Real imagery occupies large, intentional regions; text overlays are short and backed by adequate contrast.
4. Major sections change scale, color or image treatment so the page has a rhythm rather than repeated identical panels.
5. Sponsor roles are named and ranked. The most senior partner may appear alongside the event identity; smaller partners are grouped in a directory.
6. Mobile layouts recombine their parts: readable identity, visible menu, legible date and a strong action. They are not miniature desktop layouts.
7. The shortest route to the event action is visible early, while detailed logistics live on event pages.

For NEOChosen, the most promising direction combines BottleRock's photographic scale, Newport's editorial pacing, The FEST's clear quick paths and Jazz Fest's integrated top-tier sponsor recognition. The artwork, copy and interface details must remain original to NEOChosen.
