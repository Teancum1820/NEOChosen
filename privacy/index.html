Privacy & Cookies

NEOChosen is presented by Kirtland Heritage Group, an Ohio nonprofit. We use cookies and similar browser technologies, including local storage, to remember your cookie choices. With your permission, we also use optional Meta Pixel measurement to understand site visits and clicks on The Piano Guys ticket link to Ticketmaster.

Your choice

Meta’s optional cookies and tracking are off unless you choose “Accept optional cookies.” Choose “Reject optional cookies” to keep this measurement off. You can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer. Your choice is saved in this browser. If browser storage is unavailable, optional Meta tracking remains off.

What the Pixel measures

  • After you allow it, the Meta Pixel sends a page-view event while you browse this site.
  • After you allow it, clicking the Piano Guys Ticketmaster link sends a custom “TicketClick” event.
  • This site does not send ticket purchases, payment information, or checkout details through this Pixel setup.

Information shared with Meta

When enabled, Meta may receive event information and technical data such as browser, device, and page information. Meta processes that information under its own terms and privacy policy. The Pixel script is not loaded before consent.
