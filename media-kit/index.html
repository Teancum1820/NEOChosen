FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

The Chosen Cast and The Piano Guys Come to Northeast Ohio Nov. 13–15

Three-day NEOChosen Weekend includes free events in Lakewood, Fairlawn and Chesterland, a VIP donor dinner and a separately ticketed concert at Akron Civic Theatre

Media contact: Jessica Barber | Kirtland Heritage Group
(801) 390-8574 | jessica@kirtlandheritagegroup.com | NEOChosen.com

CLEVELAND, Ohio — Oct. 1, 2026 — Four cast members from The Chosen and musical group The Piano Guys will visit Northeast Ohio Nov. 13–15 for NEOChosen Weekend, a regional gathering of live music, cast conversations, meet-and-greets and community events organized by Kirtland Heritage Group and presented by Great Lakes Auto Group. Participating cast members include Shaan Sharma (Shmuel), Noah James (Andrew), Vanessa Benavente (Mother Mary) and Yasmine Al-Bustami (Ramah).

The Piano Guys will perform Saturday, Nov. 14, at 4 p.m. at Akron Civic Theatre. Tickets go on sale Friday, Oct. 2, 2026. Visit NEOChosen.com for ticket information. The weekend also includes three free public events requiring advance registration, plus a VIP donor and sponsor gathering.

Kirtland Heritage Group projects approximately 5,000 event attendances across the weekend, building on its 2025 programs featuring cast members from The Chosen, which welcomed nearly 1,800 attendees and representatives from 158 congregations across Northern Ohio.

“We want to bring families, congregations, community organizations and neighbors together for a weekend centered on faith, music, service and connection,” said Joe Jackson, president of Kirtland Heritage Group. “Our goal is to create an atmosphere where people from different communities and faith traditions feel welcome.”

NEOCHOSEN WEEKEND SCHEDULE

Friday, Nov. 13 — VIP Donor Dinner | 5 p.m. | Windows on the River, 2000 Sycamore Street, Cleveland. A donor and sponsor gathering with The Piano Guys, participating cast members from The Chosen and community guests.

Friday, Nov. 13 — Evening with Cast Members from The Chosen | 7:30 p.m.; doors at 6 p.m. | Lakewood Civic Auditorium, 14100 Franklin Blvd., Lakewood. Free admission; advance registration required at NEOChosen.com.

Saturday, Nov. 14 — The Piano Guys Live | 4 p.m.; doors at 3 p.m. | Akron Civic Theatre, 182 S. Main St., Akron. Separately ticketed concert; tickets go on sale Friday, Oct. 2. Ticket information at NEOChosen.com.

Saturday, Nov. 14 — Fairlawn Meet & Greet | 7 p.m. | St. Hilary Church, 2750 W. Market St., Fairlawn. Free admission; advance registration required at NEOChosen.com.

Sunday, Nov. 15 — Chesterland Meet & Greet | 2 p.m. | Mayfield United Methodist Church, 7747 Mayfield Road, Chesterland. Free admission; advance registration required at NEOChosen.com.

NEOChosen Weekend is presented by Great Lakes Auto Group. FNA Wealth Management presents The Piano Guys concert at Akron Civic Theatre, while Advanced Care Endodontics and FNA Wealth Management present the Fairlawn Meet & Greet. Kirtland Heritage Group is also connecting congregations, nonprofits, schools, businesses, civic leaders and volunteers throughout Northeast Ohio to support the weekend.

Registration, schedules, ticket information and announcements are available at NEOChosen.com. Event schedules and talent participation are subject to change. Media interview and event-coverage requests may be directed to Jessica Barber using the contact information above.

ABOUT KIRTLAND HERITAGE GROUP

Kirtland Heritage Group is a Northeast Ohio-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit that brings people together through faith-centered, family-friendly events, interfaith relationships, service initiatives, music and community programs. The organization works with congregations, schools, businesses, civic leaders, volunteers and nonprofit partners throughout Greater Northeast Ohio. Learn more at KirtlandHeritageGroup.com.
