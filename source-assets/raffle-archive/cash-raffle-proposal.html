How will I receive my raffle tickets?

Confirmation and numbered tickets

After a successful purchase, purchasers are expected to receive a confirmation email with individually numbered raffle tickets.

Kirtland Heritage Group will prepare and retain a matching physical drawing stub for every valid ticket purchased.

Do I need to print or bring my ticket?

Purchasers will not need to print, collect, return, or bring their customer copy to be included in the drawing. Winners do not need to be present.

Re-sending or reprinting a customer copy will not create additional entries.
