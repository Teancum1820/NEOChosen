2. Eligibility — proposed

The proposed raffle is limited to eligible individuals who:

  • Are at least 18 years old
  • Are Ohio residents
  • Are physically present in Ohio when purchasing
  • Purchase entries for themselves
  • Meet all other requirements in the final rules

Proposed exclusions include KHG officers, directors, employees, people administering the raffle, and specified family/household members.

Exact exclusions and eligibility requirements are pending legal review.

Draft · not final

5. Minimum participation

Working proposal: At least six distinct eligible purchasers are required.

If the threshold is not met, the proposed process is cancellation and refunds in accordance with the final approved rules.

“Eligible gross raffle-ticket sales” is proposed to mean eligible ticket-sales receipts before payment-processing fees or raffle expenses are deducted. This definition still requires final legal/accounting confirmation, including treatment of refunds and reversals.

Separate donations, sponsorships, event admissions, and optional Zeffy contributions are excluded from the prize pool.

The remaining 60% is proposed to be retained by Kirtland Heritage Group to cover allowable raffle expenses and support its programs.

Actual prize amounts depend on eligible sales. No minimum prize amount is guaranteed. Prize amounts are stated before any required tax withholding.

Draft · not final

7. Drawing procedure — proposed

  • Physical drawing stubs are planned, with one stub per valid ticket.
  • Ticket numbers must reconcile with purchase records.
  • The Grand Prize is drawn first; remaining prizes are drawn in descending order.
  • One person may win only one cash prize.
  • After a winner is selected, all remaining tickets owned by that person must be excluded from subsequent drawings.
  • Winners need not be present.

Exact physical-stub procedure, witnesses, reconciliation, and drawing controls remain subject to final approval.

Draft · not final

8. Odds

Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible raffle entries remaining for each drawing.

Draft · not final

9. Winner notification and claims — proposed

Working terms:

  • Initial contact targeted within two business days
  • At least three documented contact attempts on different days
  • Proposed 30-calendar-day response/documentation period

These are operating proposals, not claims about legal requirements.

Pending legal review.

Draft · not final

10. Verification and payment

Potential winners may be required to provide documentation necessary to verify identity, eligibility, ticket ownership, and required tax information.

Do not submit SSNs or sensitive identity documents through the normal public website. Any sensitive verification process must use an approved secure method.

Working payment proposal: Payment initiation within 30 calendar days after verification and required paperwork are complete, subject to legal/accounting requirements.

Draft · not final

11. Refunds and reversed payments

Refunded or reversed purchases must be handled consistently under the final rules. Invalid, refunded, or reversed entries must not remain eligible. Treatment must reconcile with ticket records and prize calculations.

Refund eligibility, timing, reversed-payment handling, and prize-pool adjustments remain unresolved and require legal/accounting review. No final refund policy is established by this draft.

Draft · not final

12. Disqualification and unclaimed prizes

Replacement-drawing procedures and lawful disposition of unclaimed amounts require final legal approval.

Unclaimed prizes do not automatically become donations.

Draft · not final

13. Cancellation or postponement

The working proposal includes:

  • Defined permitted reasons, still to be confirmed
  • Purchaser notification
  • No extension solely to increase revenue
  • Proposed maximum 30-day postponement
  • Cancellation-refund initiation within 10 business days

These are proposed operating terms subject to legal, accounting, platform, and organizational review.

Draft · not final

14. Privacy

Transaction communications are separate from marketing consent. Marketing signup must be optional. Only necessary purchaser information should be collected, and sensitive information must be handled securely.

Winner announcements must follow approved privacy/legal requirements. Private purchaser information must not be published.

Consent, retention, secure verification, and public winner-announcement procedures remain subject to approval.

Draft · not final

15. Tax notice

Raffle-ticket purchases are not tax-deductible charitable contributions.

Any separate donation must be processed separately and treated according to applicable rules.

Draft · not final

16. Ticketing platform

KHG intends to use Zeffy for online raffle-ticket transactions and ticket delivery, subject to platform confirmation and final approval.

This draft does not imply Zeffy has approved the raffle. Zeffy does not operate the drawing or determine prize amounts under this proposal.

Final ticket delivery is pending approval. Customer copies are expected to be emailed with individual ticket numbers; KHG will retain matching physical stubs. Re-sending or reprinting a customer copy will not create additional entries. Purchasers will not need to print, collect, return, or bring their copy to enter the drawing.
