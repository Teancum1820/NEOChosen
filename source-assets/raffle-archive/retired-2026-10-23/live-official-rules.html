2. Eligibility

The raffle is limited to eligible individuals who:

  • Are at least 18 years old
  • Are U.S. residents
  • Purchase entries for themselves
  • Meet all other requirements in these rules

Exclusions include KHG officers, directors, employees, people administering the raffle, and their family or household members.

Each valid ticket represents one raffle entry. Multiple-ticket packages create unique individual ticket numbers.

Purchasers buy for themselves. No transfers, resale, jointly owned entries, or purchases for minors. Multiple orders by the same person remain subject to the one-prize-per-person limit.

Entry requires a valid paid ticket. There is no fixed overall ticket cap. Early-bird offers, discount codes, complimentary entries, and bonus tickets are not offered.

5. Minimum participation

At least six distinct eligible purchasers are required.

If the threshold is not met, the raffle will be canceled and purchases refunded.

6. Prizes

Cash prizes as a percentage of eligible gross raffle-ticket sales
Prize Percentage of eligible gross raffle-ticket sales
Grand Prize 20%

Fifth Prize 3%

Total cash prizes 40%

“Eligible gross raffle-ticket sales” means eligible ticket-sales receipts before payment-processing fees or raffle expenses are deducted. Invalid, refunded, or reversed purchases are excluded from eligible sales and entry records.

Separate donations, sponsorships, event admissions, and optional Zeffy contributions are excluded from the prize pool.

The remaining 60% is retained by Kirtland Heritage Group to cover allowable raffle expenses and support its programs.

Actual prize amounts depend on eligible sales. No minimum prize amount is guaranteed. Prize amounts are stated before any required tax withholding.

7. Drawing procedure

  • Each valid ticket is entered once into the electronic drawing.
  • Ticket numbers must reconcile with purchase records.
  • Customer tickets, reports, and drawing records use the same unique ticket numbers generated by Zeffy.
  • The Grand Prize is drawn first; remaining prizes are drawn in descending order.
  • One person may win only one cash prize.
  • After a winner is selected, all remaining tickets owned by that person must be excluded from subsequent drawings.
  • Winners need not be present.
  • Tickets from multiple purchases by the same person are also excluded from subsequent drawings after that person wins.
  • Each valid ticket is included once. Reprinting or resending a customer copy does not create an additional entry.

8. Odds

Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible raffle entries remaining for each drawing.

9. Winner notification and claims

  • Initial contact targeted within two business days
  • At least three documented contact attempts on different days
  • 30-calendar-day response/documentation period

10. Verification and payment

Potential winners may be required to provide documentation necessary to verify identity, eligibility, ticket ownership, and required tax information.

Do not submit SSNs or sensitive identity documents through the normal public website. Any sensitive verification process must use an approved secure method.

Payment initiation within 30 calendar days after verification and required paperwork are complete, subject to legal/accounting requirements.

11. Refunds and reversed payments

Refunded or reversed purchases must be handled consistently with these rules. Invalid, refunded, or reversed entries must not remain eligible. Treatment must reconcile with ticket records and prize calculations.

12. Disqualification and unclaimed prizes

Potential winners must meet the eligibility, verification, and claim requirements in these rules. Contact KHG regarding a prize claim.

Unclaimed prizes do not automatically become donations.

13. Cancellation or postponement

In the event of cancellation or postponement:

  • Purchaser notification
  • No extension solely to increase revenue
  • Maximum 30-day postponement
  • Cancellation-refund initiation within 10 business days

14. Privacy

Transaction communications are separate from marketing consent. Marketing signup is optional. Only necessary purchaser information is collected, and sensitive information is handled securely.

Winner announcements follow applicable privacy requirements. Private purchaser information is not published.

Marketing consent is separate, optional, and unchecked by default. Following or sharing on social media is optional and does not provide extra entries or affect eligibility or odds.

15. Tax notice

Raffle-ticket purchases are not tax-deductible charitable contributions.

Any separate donation must be processed separately and treated according to applicable rules.

16. Ticketing platform

KHG uses Zeffy for online raffle-ticket transactions and ticket delivery.

KHG conducts the drawing and calculates and pays the prizes separately. Zeffy does not conduct the drawing, calculate the six-prize allocation, or pay winners.

After a successful purchase, you’ll receive confirmation by email with access to your individually numbered raffle tickets. Each valid ticket is entered into the electronic drawing. You do not need to print, collect, return, or bring a ticket, and winners do not need to be present. Re-sending or reprinting a customer copy does not create additional entries.

Event check-in or a QR-code scan is not required for a valid purchased raffle ticket to be included. A raffle ticket is not admission to any concert, dinner, devotional, or meet-and-greet.

Zeffy may suggest an optional contribution to support its platform. Purchasers can change the suggested amount or choose not to contribute. This is separate from the raffle-ticket purchase, provides no additional entries, and is excluded from cash-prize calculations.

17. Questions and disputes

Contact Kirtland Heritage Group with questions about these rules, your entries, or a disputed purchase: info@kirtlandheritagegroup.com or 440-796-1642.
