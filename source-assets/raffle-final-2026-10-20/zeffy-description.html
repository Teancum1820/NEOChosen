NEO Chosen Cash Raffle
Six Cash Prizes. One Great Cause.

Support Kirtland Heritage Group’s community, historical, charitable, and interfaith programs throughout Greater Northeast Ohio.

Ticket packages: $20 for 1 individually numbered ticket; $50 for 3 individually numbered tickets; $100 for 7 individually numbered tickets — Best Value.

Six cash prizes total 40% of eligible gross raffle-ticket sales: grand prize 20%, second 6%, third 5%, fourth 4%, fifth 3%, and sixth 2%. Actual prize amounts depend on eligible sales; no minimum dollar prize is guaranteed. KHG retains the remaining 60% before allowable raffle expenses. Limit one cash prize per person.

Sales close Sunday, November 15, 2026, at 5:00 PM Eastern Time. The drawing begins that evening at 6:00 PM Eastern Time. Winners do not need to be present.

After a successful purchase, you’ll receive confirmation by email with access to your individually numbered raffle tickets. Each valid ticket is entered into the electronic drawing. You do not need to print, collect, return, or bring a ticket, and winners do not need to be present.

Use the View tickets button in your confirmation email. The Grand Prize is drawn first, followed by the remaining prizes in descending order. After a person wins, all their remaining tickets, including tickets from separate purchases, are excluded from subsequent drawings.

Eligibility: Entries must be purchased by eligible individuals for themselves: U.S. residents age 18 or older who meet the raffle rules. Read the current raffle rules.

A raffle ticket is not admission to a concert, dinner, devotional, or meet-and-greet. Explore other NEO Chosen Weekend events.

Raffle-ticket purchases are not tax-deductible charitable contributions. Separate donations, sponsorships, event admissions, and optional contributions to Zeffy are excluded from the prize calculation. Zeffy may suggest an optional contribution to support its platform. You can change the amount or choose not to contribute. This contribution does not provide additional raffle entries and is excluded from the prize calculation.
