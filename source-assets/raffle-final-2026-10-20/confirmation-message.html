Thank you for entering the NEO Chosen Cash Raffle and supporting Kirtland Heritage Group!

Your raffle tickets
Use Zeffy’s View tickets button in this email to access all of your individually numbered raffle tickets. Your order summary and transaction receipt are included in this confirmation. Each $20 package includes 1 ticket, each $50 package includes 3 tickets, and each $100 package includes 7 tickets. Multiple packages include the combined number of tickets.

After a successful purchase, you’ll receive confirmation by email with access to your individually numbered raffle tickets. Each valid ticket is entered into the electronic drawing. You do not need to print, collect, return, or bring a ticket, and winners do not need to be present.

Save your confirmation and ticket information. Re-sending or reprinting a customer copy does not create additional entries. The Grand Prize is drawn first, followed by the remaining prizes in descending order. After a person wins, all their remaining tickets, including tickets from separate purchases, are excluded from subsequent drawings.

Drawing details
Ticket sales close Sunday, November 15, 2026, at 5:00 PM Eastern Time. The drawing begins that evening at 6:00 PM Eastern Time.

Six cash prizes total 40% of eligible gross raffle-ticket sales: 20%, 6%, 5%, 4%, 3%, and 2%. Actual prize amounts depend on eligible sales; no minimum dollar prize is guaranteed. Limit one cash prize per person.

Eligibility and rules
U.S. residents age 18 or older, purchasing for themselves and meeting the raffle rules.
