Good to know

Your tickets. Your questions.

How will I receive my tickets?

After your purchase, you’ll receive an email confirmation with your individually numbered raffle tickets. Kirtland Heritage Group retains a matching physical drawing stub for each valid ticket.

Do I need to print or bring my ticket?

No. You do not need to print, collect, return, or bring your customer copy to be included in the drawing. Winners do not need to be present. Re-sending or reprinting a customer copy does not create additional entries.

Can I win more than one prize?

There is a limit of one cash prize per person. Once a person wins, their remaining entries are excluded from subsequent prize drawings.

What determines the prize amounts?

Each prize is a percentage of eligible gross raffle-ticket sales. Separate donations, sponsorships, event admissions, and optional Zeffy contributions are not part of the prize pool. See the raffle rules for the complete terms.
