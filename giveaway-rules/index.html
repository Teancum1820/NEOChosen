Official Giveaway Rules

NEOChosen Front-Row Seat Giveaway

Prize Four reserved front-row seats
Event November 13, 2026 at 7:30 p.m.
Deadline September 27 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern
Entry Comment with your favorite character from The Chosen

Organizer

This giveaway is organized by Kirtland Heritage Group.

The giveaway is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Meta, Facebook, or Instagram.

Prize

One winner will receive four reserved front-row seats for the NEOChosen event at Lakewood Civic Auditorium on Friday, November 13, 2026 at 7:30 p.m. The winner may bring three guests.

The prize includes only the four reserved front-row seats.

The prize does not include:

  • Backstage access or private meetings with cast members
  • Dinner, transportation, lodging, or meals
  • Cash alternatives
  • Any other benefit not specifically listed above

Giveaway Period

The giveaway begins when the official giveaway posts are published and closes Sunday, September 27, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time.

The random drawing will begin on Monday, September 28, 2026.

Eligibility

The giveaway is open to adults age 18 or older who reside in one of these Ohio counties:

  • Lake County
  • Geauga County
  • Cuyahoga County
  • Summit County
  • Medina County
  • Portage County

People administering the giveaway and members of their immediate households are not eligible. Existing Kirtland Heritage Group followers are eligible.

No purchase, donation, ticket registration, follow, like, tag, or share is required to enter. A purchase or donation does not improve the chances of winning.

How to Enter

Eligible participants may enter by commenting on an official giveaway post with their favorite character from The Chosen.

  • One eligible entry through the official Instagram giveaway post
  • One eligible entry through the official Facebook giveaway post

Maximum: two total entries per person across the entire giveaway—one on Instagram and one on Facebook.

Repeated comments do not create additional entries. Tags, shares, likes, follows, reposts, comments from additional accounts, and additional comments do not create bonus entries.

Following or sharing may be encouraged, but they are optional and do not improve a person's chances of winning. On Instagram, participants may voluntarily tag a friend, but tagging is not required and does not create another entry.

Winner Selection

One winner will be selected at random from the combined pool of eligible Instagram and Facebook entries.

The potential winner will be contacted beginning Monday, September 28, 2026 through an official Kirtland Heritage Group account. Eligibility and acceptance must be confirmed before the winner is publicly announced.

The selected winner will have 48 hours after official notification to respond. Another eligible entry may be selected at random if the selected person:

  • Does not respond within 48 hours
  • Is ineligible
  • Declines the prize
  • Cannot verify eligibility

Winner Verification

Kirtland Heritage Group may request only the information reasonably necessary to confirm age, confirm eligible county of residence, confirm identity, and deliver the prize.

Entrants will not be added to a mailing list unless they separately consent. Kirtland Heritage Group will never require payment or credit-card information to claim the prize.

General Admission

General admission to the event is free, subject to availability. This giveaway is specifically for four reserved front-row seats. Winning is not required to attend through general admission.

After the giveaway is completed, non-winners may visit NEOChosen.com for event information and available general-admission reservations.

Platform Disclaimer

This giveaway is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Meta, Facebook, or Instagram. By entering, participants acknowledge that they are providing their information to Kirtland Heritage Group and not to Meta, Facebook, or Instagram.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, each entrant releases and holds harmless Meta, Facebook, and Instagram from claims arising out of or related to this giveaway.

Acceptance of Rules

By participating, each entrant accepts these official rules and the decisions of Kirtland Heritage Group regarding the administration of the giveaway.
